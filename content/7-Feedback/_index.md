Here's what I really think about my time at First Cloud AI Journey. Hope it helps the FCAJ team make things even better for the next batches.

### Overall

**1. Working Environment**

The vibe at FCAJ is open, friendly, and professional. The workspace at Bitexco Tower is comfortable, got everything you need for learning and building. What I liked most was the community feel, being around people who are just as into Cloud & AI as you are creates this energy for learning that I never felt in a classroom.

**2. Support from Team Admin**  

The admin team was on top of things like scheduling, docs, and logistics. Slack and Discord were always buzzing, so questions rarely sat for more than a few hours. The program is mostly self-learning, which pushed me to figure things out and research stuff on my own. That's a real skill you need for actual work later. What I liked most was the freedom to explore and try things out instead of just following a script.

**3. How It Matches My Major**

The work fit pretty well with my IT major: AWS architecture, Python, databases, networking, MLOps. But it also pushed me way past what school teaches, into stuff like Agentic AI, hackathons, writing technical blogs, and community stuff. This mix of reinforcing basics while learning new things was just what I needed.

**4. Learning & Skill Growth**

In 8 weeks, I learned things school doesn't teach: building production-grade ML pipelines, co-authoring 3 technical blogs (database connections, security, IaC with Terraform), presenting at community events, and working as a team under hackathon pressure. The program structure — learn, build, share — worked really well.

**5. Community & Team Spirit**

The community is FCAJ's strongest thing. From meetup speakers to other interns, everyone shared what they knew openly. The show-and-tell sessions, group blog discussions, and hackathon collab made it feel like everyone was growing together rather than competing.

**6. Perks & Policies for Interns**

The program gave us free access to the learning platform (cloudjourney.awsstudygroup.com), AWS credits for hands-on, a structured learning path, and tons of chances to connect with industry people. The flexible schedule let me balance labs, events, and writing reports without too much stress.

---

### A Few More Questions

**What was the most satisfying part?**

Two things. First, building the MLOps Pipeline from zero and watching it actually run: processing data, training a model, deploying, and getting real predictions through an API. Second, standing up at FCAJ Community Day on stage. That moment of realizing "I just explained our team's work to a room full of people and they got it" was something I won't forget.

**What should the company improve for future interns?**

- A clearer list of which AWS services are free and which cost money. Some of us accidentally got small bills.
- A few more check-ins in the first couple weeks so new interns feel more supported.
- Maybe a buddy system where new interns get paired with someone from a past cohort.

**Would you tell a friend to intern here? Why?**

Yeah, 100%. FCAJ isn't one of those "sit and watch" internships. You jump in from day one, it's community-driven, and it's built to push you past your comfort zone. If someone is actually serious about Cloud & AI and ready to put in the work, this program will speed up their growth more than any course or tutorial.

---

### Ideas & Hopes

- **Making things smoother:** A short workshop at the start on "GitHub + Hugo + Markdown" would help interns get their reports set up faster instead of struggling with the tools.
- **Continuing the program:** I'd love to come back as an alumni or mentor for future FCAJ batches. The program changed a lot for me and I'd be happy to give back.
- **Last thought:** Thanks to the whole FCAJ team: the admins, event organizers, and the community. These 8 weeks have been the most intense and rewarding learning period of my student life so far.
