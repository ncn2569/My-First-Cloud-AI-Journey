Here, I'd like to share my honest opinions about my experience at First Cloud AI Journey, hoping to help the FCAJ team improve for future cohorts.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

The environment at FCAJ is open, friendly, and professional. The workspace at Bitexco Tower is comfortable, with full equipment for learning and building. What I appreciated most was the community atmosphere — working alongside people passionate about Cloud & AI created a powerful learning energy that I had never experienced in a classroom setting.

**2. Support from Team Admin**  

The admin team was very supportive with scheduling, documentation, and logistics. The Slack/Discord channels were active, so questions rarely went more than a few hours without a response. The program follows a self-learning model — which pushed me to proactively research and solve problems on my own, a highly valuable skill for real-world work. I especially appreciated the freedom to explore and experiment rather than just following step-by-step instructions.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**

The work aligned well with my Information Technology studies — AWS architecture, Python, databases, networking, MLOps — but also pushed me far beyond the curriculum into areas like Agentic AI, hackathons, technical writing, and community engagement. This blend of reinforcement and expansion was exactly what I needed.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**

In 8 weeks, I picked up skills I wouldn't have touched in university: building production-grade ML pipelines, co-authoring technical blogs, presenting at community events, and collaborating under hackathon pressure. The program's structure — alternating between studying, building, and sharing — was extremely effective.

**5. Community Culture & Team Spirit**

The community spirit is FCAJ's strongest asset. From meetup speakers to fellow interns, everyone shared knowledge openly without gatekeeping. The Friday show-and-tell sessions, group blog discussions, and hackathon collaboration created a genuine sense of collective growth rather than individual competition.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**

The program provided free access to the learning platform (cloudjourney.awsstudygroup.com), AWS credits for hands-on practice, a structured learning roadmap, and countless networking opportunities with industry professionals. The flexible schedule allowed me to balance hands-on labs, events, and report writing effectively.

### Additional Questions

**What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**

Two things: first, building the MLOps Pipeline from scratch and seeing it actually work — processing data, training a model, deploying it, and getting real predictions through an API. Second, standing up at FCAJ Community Day as a speaker for the first time. That moment of realizing "I just explained our work to a room full of people and they got it" was unforgettable.

**What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**

- Provide clearer documentation on which AWS services are in Free Tier and which incur costs — some interns accidentally racked up small bills.
- Schedule a few more 1-on-1 check-ins in the early weeks to help interns feel more supported.
- Consider adding an optional "buddy system" pairing new interns with previous cohort members.

**If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why?**

Absolutely yes. FCAJ is not a typical "sit and watch" internship. It's hands-on from day one, community-driven, and designed to push you beyond your comfort zone. If someone is serious about Cloud & AI and willing to put in the effort, this program will accelerate their growth faster than any course or tutorial could.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- **Improving the internship experience:** A short introductory workshop on "GitHub + Hugo + Markdown" at the start would help interns get their reports set up faster, reducing the learning curve for documentation.
- **Continuing the program:** I would love to stay involved as an alumni or mentor for future FCAJ cohorts. The program has been transformative for me, and I'd be happy to give back.
- **Other thoughts:** Thank you to the entire FCAJ team — team admin, event organizers, and the community. These 8 weeks have been the most intensive and rewarding learning period of my academic journey so far.
