### Areas for Improvement

* **Communication:** Need to be more concise when presenting technical topics, especially to non-technical audiences.
* **Critical thinking:** Need to ask more "why" questions when designing solutions, not just follow instructions.
* **Time management:** Some tasks were rushed toward the end of the week — need more even distribution of effort.
* **Testing mindset:** Need to write tests earlier in the development cycle, not wait until the end.
