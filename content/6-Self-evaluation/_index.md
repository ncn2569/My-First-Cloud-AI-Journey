### Stuff I need to work on

* **Communication:** Need to get better at explaining things shorter and clearer, especially to people who aren't technical.
* **Critical thinking:** Should ask more "why" questions when designing solutions instead of just doing what the guide says.
* **Time management:** Some tasks got crammed into the end of the week, need to spread things out more evenly.
* **Testing mindset:** Should write tests earlier while building, not wait till everything is done.
